# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want YoBook API to be welcoming, respectful, and useful for people who care about education access, open data, and public learning resources.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive
- Assume good intent
- Credit original sources and contributors
- Respect copyright and source terms
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing private or restricted content
- Removing attribution or source notices
- Misrepresenting third-party content as your own
- Spam or unrelated promotion

## Reporting

If something feels wrong, open an issue or contact the maintainers through the repository's listed contact method.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code of conduct.
